# Numerical studies

These reproducible assessments document numerical design choices in OpenONDA.
Each study states its model, assumptions, evidence and limits, and includes
the scripts, recorded results and figures needed to inspect the conclusions.

- [VPM time integration](vpm_time_integration/README.md): temporal accuracy
  of common-stage position/strength integration, compared with the historical
  sequential update and a symmetric split in controlled particle systems.
